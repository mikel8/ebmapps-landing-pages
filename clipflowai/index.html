Turn one app demo into ready-to-share short-form content.

Upload an MP4, MOV, or WebM demo, describe the app, and generate five hook and caption ideas plus one vertical clip in the current MVP.

Current MVP workflow
  1. Choose a video up to 200 MB.
  2. Enter app context and optional hook styles.
  3. Generate five copy ideas with AI.
  4. Render one 9:16 MP4 clip and download the full ZIP export.

What ClipFlow AI does not do

The Chrome extension does not connect to TikTok, use social-platform OAuth, or publish content. It prepares files and copy for your review.
