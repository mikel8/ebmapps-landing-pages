Data Deletion

Last updated: July 9, 2026

You can request deletion of any data associated with your use of ClipFlow AI at any time. This page explains what data exists and how to remove it.

What data ClipFlow AI holds

  • TikTok OAuth tokens — Stored locally on your device only. Never held on ClipFlow AI servers.
  • Basic TikTok profile info — Username and display name, cached in-app to show the connected account. Not stored externally.
  • Operational logs — Basic error logs for troubleshooting, retained temporarily.

ClipFlow AI does not store video content, captions, or TikTok credentials on external servers. Videos are submitted directly to TikTok on your approval and are not retained by ClipFlow AI.

How to delete your data

1
Revoke TikTok access Go to your TikTok account → Settings → Security → Authorized Apps → remove ClipFlow AI. This immediately revokes all OAuth tokens.
2
Uninstall the app Uninstalling ClipFlow AI removes all locally stored tokens and cached profile data from your device.
3
Request log deletion (optional) To request deletion of any operational logs associated with your account, email us at michaellu8@gmail.com with the subject line "ClipFlow AI Data Deletion Request". We will process your request within 30 days.

Contact

For data deletion requests or questions: michaellu8@gmail.com

We will confirm receipt of your request and complete deletion within 30 days.
