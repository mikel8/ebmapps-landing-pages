Data Deletion

Last updated: July 9, 2026

You can request deletion of any data associated with your use of ClipFlow AI Studio at any time. This page explains what data exists and how to remove it.

What data ClipFlow AI Studio holds

  • Uploaded video and app context — Held temporarily by the processing service while a request is processed and while download links are available.
  • Operational logs — Basic error logs for troubleshooting, retained temporarily.

ClipFlow AI Studio does not use TikTok OAuth, store TikTok credentials, or publish content to TikTok. Temporary media and generated results are not an account library or permanent storage.

How to delete your data

1
Request deletion of processed data Email us at michaellu8@gmail.com with the approximate date and original filename. We will review and process the request within 30 days.
2
Remove local extension data Uninstalling ClipFlow AI Studio removes locally stored extension data from your device.
3
Request log deletion (optional) To request deletion of any operational logs associated with your account, email us at michaellu8@gmail.com with the subject line "ClipFlow AI Studio Data Deletion Request". We will process your request within 30 days.

Contact

For data deletion requests or questions: michaellu8@gmail.com

We will confirm receipt of your request and complete deletion within 30 days.
