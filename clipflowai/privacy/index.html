Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. Scope and contact

This policy explains how EBM Apps handles information when you use the ClipFlow AI Chrome extension and its video-processing service. ClipFlow AI helps users prepare short-form video content; it does not connect to TikTok, use TikTok OAuth, or publish to social platforms.

Privacy questions and deletion requests: michaellu8@gmail.com.

2. Information processed

  • Video files: The video you choose is uploaded to the ClipFlow AI processing service to create a vertical clip.
  • App context: The app name, category, what the video shows, target user, main pain point, selected hook styles, and original filename are sent with the upload so generated copy matches your project.
  • Generated results: The service creates hook copy, captions, hashtags, a call to action, a rendered MP4 clip, and a ZIP export.
  • Technical information: The service may receive ordinary request metadata such as IP address, timestamps, and error logs from its hosting provider for security and troubleshooting.

Do not upload confidential, sensitive, or personal video content unless you are authorized to send it to the service.

3. How information is used

  • To generate five hook and caption ideas from the supplied app context.
  • To render the current MVP's one vertical MP4 clip and assemble the downloadable ZIP.
  • To return results, diagnose failures, prevent abuse, and maintain the service.

ClipFlow AI does not use submitted videos or app context for advertising, does not sell them, and does not use them to publish content to TikTok or another social platform.

4. OpenAI and other service providers

To generate hooks and social copy, ClipFlow AI sends the original filename and the app context listed above to OpenAI through the backend. The video is processed by the ClipFlow AI backend with FFmpeg; the current implementation does not send the video file to OpenAI.

ClipFlow AI also uses Render for hosting and delivery. These providers process information only as needed to operate the service and may generate ordinary infrastructure logs under their own policies. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the service and its users.

5. Storage, retention, and deletion

The backend writes the uploaded source video, rendered clip, and ZIP to temporary service storage while a request is processed and while its download links are available. The current service does not provide an account library or permanent media storage. Temporary files are subject to the hosting environment's filesystem lifecycle and may remain until the service restarts or storage is cleaned; users should treat generated links as temporary.

ClipFlow AI does not retain TikTok credentials because it does not use TikTok authentication. To request deletion of any operational data associated with a request, email michaellu8@gmail.com with the approximate date and original filename. We will review and process the request within 30 days, subject to legal and security requirements.

6. Your choices and security

You choose whether to upload a file and what information to enter. You can stop using the extension at any time and delete downloaded results from your device. We use HTTPS for browser-to-service communication and limit access to the service configuration, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children and changes

ClipFlow AI is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. We may update this policy; the date above shows when it was last revised.
