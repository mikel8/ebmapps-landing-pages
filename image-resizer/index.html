Questions, answered

Image Resizer FAQ

Do I need a subscription to resize or compress photos?

No. Core editing features are free. The optional one-time purchase removes ads.

Which image formats can I create?

You can prepare output as JPEG, PNG, or WebP.

Can I process more than one photo?

Yes. Batch processing applies a consistent recipe to multiple selected images.

Can I reuse the same settings?

Yes. Save a workflow and run the same resize, compression, format, and destination setup again later.

Where are my photos processed?

Image processing happens on your device. The app does not need to upload your photos to an image-processing server.
