Privacy Policy

This page covers EBM Apps products. Existing product-specific policies remain available on their product pages.

BlogToBook

BlogToBook downloads and processes the public blog or newsletter archive you choose to build the requested book. In the Mac app, article and image caches remain on your Mac. In the hosted web app, selected public article content, images, job state, and generated EPUB files are processed and stored on BlogToBook's server as needed to provide the service and its download workflow.

BlogToBook uses PostHog for anonymous product analytics such as app launch, inspection outcomes, crawl outcomes, review usage, and EPUB export outcomes. Events may include aggregate properties such as app version, discovery strategy, counts, image counts, artifact size, completeness, and failure categories. An anonymous installation or browser identifier is used; no BlogToBook account is required.

BlogToBook does not send submitted archive URLs, article URLs, titles, scraped article content, HTML, images, or EPUB contents to PostHog. Stripe processes payments for Founding Access. For purchase recovery, BlogToBook uses the verified email collected by Stripe to send a short-lived, single-use verification code; the service stores a one-way email hash rather than the email address in its entitlement records.
